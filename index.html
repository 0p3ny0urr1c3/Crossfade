Genres come from Last.fm's community tags on each member's most-played artists. Last.fm doesn't expose tempo or audio-feature data, so matching is based on tag overlap, listening rank, and Last.fm's own "similar artists / similar tracks" signals rather than BPM or key. Recommended tracks are limited to songs that don't already appear in anyone's top 100.
